Head of Product

Remote (US) · Product · Full-time

applyapply finds the jobs that fit a person and writes the application with them: a tailored resume, a cover note, and answers to every question on the form. The candidate reviews it and submits it themselves.

What you'll do

  • Own the product from sourcing through submission, across the web app, the browser extension and the agent API.
  • Decide what gets written by a model and what gets selected, and make both feel instant.
  • Talk to job seekers every week and turn what you hear into shipped changes.

You might be a fit if

  • You have taken an AI product from first research to real, repeated use.
  • You measure outcomes, not output, and can say what you chose not to build.
